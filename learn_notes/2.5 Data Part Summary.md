## Challenges

- The trade-off(权衡) between label quality vs data volume(数据量)
- Data quality:
  - diversity(多样性): all relevant(有关的) aspects(方面) are represented
  - unbiased(无偏的): no biased on a particular(特定的) side
  - fairness: non discriminating(歧视) treatment(对待) of data and people
- Large-scale data management: storage, process, versioning(版本控制), security
