## Image Transformations

- Downsampling(下采样) and cropping(裁剪)
  - Reduce image sizes to save storage(存储) and for fast loading
  -  ML is good at low-resolution(低分辨率) images
  - Be aware(注意) of (jpeg) image quality
- Image whitening
  - Make input less redundant(冗余)
  - Model converges(收敛) faster

## Video Transformations

- Average(平均) video length
- Preprocessing to balance storage, quality and loading speed
- We often use short video clips(片段) (<10sec)
  - Each clip usually contains a single(单一的) event
- Decode(解码) a playable video, sample(采样) a sequence(序列) of frames
  - Best for loading, but 10x more space
  - Computation may be cheaper than storage
    <font color="blue">eg: use GPU to decode</font>
  - Can apply other image transformations to the frames

## Text Transformations

- Stemming(词干提取) and lemmatization(词形还原): a word → a common base form
  - <font color="blue">E.g.am, are, is - be   car, cars, car's, cars - car</font>
- Tokenization(词元化): text → a list of tokens (smallest unit(单位) to ML algorithms)
  - Byword: text.split (' ')· 
  - Bychar: text.split('')·
  - By subwords(子词):
    - Unigram(一元模型), WordPiece,...
    - <font color="blue">e.g."anew gpu" → "a", "new", "gp", "##u", "!"</font>
