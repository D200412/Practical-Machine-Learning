# 5.1 Bias(偏差) & Variance(方差)

- In statistic(统计的) learning, we measure a model in terms for bias and variance

<font color="blue">偏差：学习到的模型真实模型之间的区别位移</font>
<font color="blue">方差：每次学习的东西差别多大</font>

## Bias-Variance Decomposition(分解)

- Sample data $D=\{(x_1,y_1),...,(x_n,y_n)\}$ from $y=f(x)+\varepsilon $
- Learn $\hat{f}$ from $D$ by minimizing MSE, we want it generates well over different choices of $D$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_D\left[(y-\hat{f}(x))^2\right]
&= \mathbb{E}\left[\left((f-\mathbb{E}[\hat{f}])+\varepsilon-(\hat{f}-\mathbb{E}[\hat{f}])\right)^2\right] \\
&= (f-\mathbb{E}[\hat{f}])^2+\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon^2]
+\mathbb{E}\left[(\hat{f}-\mathbb{E}[\hat{f}])^2\right] \\
&= \operatorname{Bias}[\hat{f}]^2+\operatorname{Var}[\hat{f}]+\sigma^2
\end{aligned}
$$

## Bias-Variance Tradeoff

![image-20260903164638276](http://look-this-world.oss-cn-qingdao.aliyuncs.com/HelloWorld/image-20260903164638276.png)

当模型很简单的时候，真实之间的偏差是比较远的。随着模型越来越复制，很可能会学到真实的值，同时，能够拟合的东西越多，过多的去看了一些噪音，导致方差越来越大。

## Reduce Bias & Variance

- Reduce bias
  - A more complex model
    - E.g. increase layers, hidden units neural network
    - <font color="ca6924">Boosting</font>
    - <font color="ca6924">Stacking</font>
- Reduce vatiance
  - A simple model
  - Regularization
    - E.g $L_1,L_2$ reguiarizations
    - <font color="ca6924">Bagging</font>
    - <font color="ca6924">Stacking</font>
- Reduce $\sigma^2$
  - Improve data

<font color="ca6924">Ensemble(集成) learning</font>: use multiple models toimprove predictive performance
