# 9.2 HPO algorithms

## Search Space

- Specify(指定) range for each hyperparameter
- The search space can be exponentially(指数级的) large
  - Need to carefully design the space to improve efficiency

## HPO algorithms:Black-box or Multi-fidelity

- Black-box: treats a training job as a black-box in HPO:
  - Completes the training process for each trial(实验)

- Multi-fidelity: modifies the training job to speed up the search
  - Train on subsampled datasets
  - Reduce model size (e.g less #layers, #channels)
  - Stop bad configuration(配置) earlier

## Two most common HPO strategies(策略)

- **Grid search(网格搜索)**

  ```python
  for config in search_space:
  	train_and_eval(config)
  return best_result
  ```

  - All combinations are evaluated(评估)
  - Guarantees(保证) the best results
  - Curse of dimensionality(维度)

- **Random search(随机搜索)**

  ```python
  for _ in range(n):
  	config = random_select(search_space)
  	train_and_eval(config)
  return best_result
  ```

  - Random combinations,
  - More efficient than grid(empirically and in theory, shown in [Random Search for Hyper-Parameter Optimization)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume13/bergstra12a/bergstra12a.pdf)

## Bayesian Optimization(贝叶斯优化) (BO)

- <font color="ca6924">BO</font>: Iteratively(迭代的) learn a mapping from HP(超参数) to objective function.
  Based on previous trials(实验). Select the next trial based on the current(当前) estimation(估计).
- <font color="ca6924">Surrogate model</font>
  - Estimate how the objective function depends on HP了。
  - Probabilistic(概率的) regression models: Ranaom forest, Gaussian process

- <font color="ca6924">Acquisition(获取) function</font>
  - Acquisition max means uncertainty and predicted objective are high.
  - Sample the next trial according to the acquisition function
  - Trade off exploration(探索) and exploitation(挖掘)
- <font color="ca6924">Limitation(限制) of BO</font>:
  - In the initial(初始) stages, similar to random search
  - Optimization process is sequential(顺序的)

## Successive(连续) Halving(减半)

- Save the budget(预算) for most promising config(配置)
- Randomly pick $n$ configurations to train $m$ epochs
- Repeat until one configuration left:
  - Keep the **best** $n/2$ configuration to train another $m$ epochs
  - Keep the **best** $n/4$ configuration to train another $2m$ epochs
  - ……
- Select $n$ and $m$ based on training budget and #epoch needed for a full training

## Hyperband

- In Successive Halving
  - $n$: exploration
  - $m$: exploitation\
- Hyperband runs multiple Successive Halving, eachtime decreases $n$ and increases $m$
  - More exploration first, then do more exploit
